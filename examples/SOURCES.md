# Sources and scenario construction

**FoodGo Test Dataset.** Z. B. Rubinstein, Carnegie Mellon University. Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source archive contains 500 orders and 50 volunteers. This adaptation preserves the original order and volunteer numbers, the selected pickup and delivery locations, quantity units, capacities and shifts. It selects orders 24–26, converts local times to minutes after midnight and adds dispatch conditions below. The archive MD5 published by Zenodo is `82b315217cec5c9b24650edbdab9c2d9`. [Dataset and files](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3801643) · [US Department of Transportation catalogue](https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/56260)

**Driving data.** © OpenStreetMap contributors. The saved [road matrix](roads.json) is provided under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The generator queried the public OSRM driving table for 30 benchmark locations and rounded seconds up to minutes. Travel times are directed estimates. Source points snapped up to 208 metres to the routable network in this snapshot. The timestamp of retrieval is in the file. Traffic, parking and actual arrival times are unobserved. [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [OSRM Table service](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/#table-service)

**Simulated cancellation at 15:30 in Pittsburgh.** The example treats FoodGo starting points as volunteer check-in locations, uses a 15-minute freshness threshold, books two fixed appointments, adds existing loads for V23 and V29 and marks V30's check-in stale. V49 cancelled. V28 lacks a cold carrier. Each end of a trip takes five minutes to handle. R24 has an illustrative 55-minute transport limit, R25 has a tightened receiving deadline and R26 is made perishable with a missing time limit. These are scenario inputs, including the transport limit. An operating fund must provide its own current handling policies and verified inputs. Units are the benchmark's generic quantities.

**Rebuild.** Run `python scripts/refresh_demo.py` from the root to download FoodGo and refresh both JSON files. This sends public benchmark coordinates to OSRM. New map data can change the sample outputs. The checked-in files reproduce the recorded comparison offline.
